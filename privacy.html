Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Merchant's Bluff

This Privacy Policy explains how Merchant's Bluff (the "App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. The short version: the App does not collect personal data from you and does not transmit anything off your device. The rest of this document spells out what permissions we ask for, what is stored locally, and how to reach us.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Merchant's Bluff does not request, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or contact list.
  • Account credentials, sign-in tokens, or authentication cookies.
  • Device identifiers for advertising or tracking.
  • Location data, IP address, or browsing history.
  • Crash reports or analytics events.

The App runs entirely on-device and is fully usable without an internet connection.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following non-personal data locally in the iOS app container. You can erase this data at any time using the in-app Settings → Erase All Data option, by deleting the App, or by offloading the App from iOS Settings.

  • Player profile: Display name and optional avatar image (camera capture or photo-library selection).
  • Game progress: Round outcomes, lifetime statistics, and unlocked trophies.
  • Preferences: Sound effects on/off, haptic feedback on/off, and onboarding state.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it (for example, by saving a snapshot of your trophy room to your photo library).

3. Permissions We Request

Merchant's Bluff only requests iOS permissions that are strictly necessary for an optional feature. All are gated behind an explicit in-app prompt.

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used solely to capture an avatar image for your player profile. Photos stay on this device.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used solely to select an avatar image for your player profile.
  • Save to Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription): Used solely when you choose to export a prize snapshot or trophy room screenshot to your camera roll.

You may decline any of these permissions; the App remains fully playable with the default heraldic crest.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising, social-login, or crash-reporting SDKs. There are no third parties with access to your data through the App.

5. Children

Merchant's Bluff is suitable for ages 12 and up and does not knowingly collect any information from children. Because no information is collected at all, the App is inherently safe for younger audiences within the recommended age range.

6. Data Security

All stored data is contained within the iOS sandbox for this App. The App does not use network connections, so there is no data in transit to protect. Camera and photo-library access follow the standard iOS permission model.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy (for example, to add a clearly opt-in cloud-save feature), we will update the "Effective date" above and, where required, surface the change inside the App before it applies.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to access, export, or delete on a remote server. All data lives on your device and is fully under your control via the in-app Erase All Data option or by deleting the App.

9. Virtual Items Disclaimer

All tokens, prizes, and rewards in Merchant's Bluff are virtual. They have no monetary value, cannot be redeemed for real currency or prizes, and exist purely as scoring markers within the game. Merchant's Bluff is a game of skill with no element of real-money play.
