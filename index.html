Merchant's Bluff revives the bluff-and-bid spirit of Mercante in Fiera, the centuries-old Italian card game played around family tables and village squares. Five prizes are tied to eight secret numbers. Read the room, place your cards, and outwit two colorful AI opponents — Signiorina Bianca and Maestro Aurelio.

How a Round Unfolds

Every match is three rounds. Every round is three acts.

01

Bidding

Eight cards land in your hand. Tap them onto the five prizes you'd like to claim — but each card can only be used once, and you won't see the merchant's sleeve.

02

The Merchant's Table

The merchant offers buys, sells, swaps, and the occasional peek at a prize's winning numbers. Some offers are honest. Some are bluffs.

03

Reveal

Each prize flips one at a time. The highest matching card wins. Highest score across three rounds takes the match.

Offline by design

Your data, your table.

Merchant's Bluff does not collect personal data. There is no analytics SDK, no tracking pixel, and no remote server. Your avatar, your trophies, your preferences — they live on your device, and only when you choose to export a snapshot do we touch the camera roll.
