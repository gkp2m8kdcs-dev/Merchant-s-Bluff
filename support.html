Frequently asked

Do I need an account to play?

No. Merchant's Bluff has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server. Open the app and play.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Everything is bundled. You can play in airplane mode on a flight.

Why does the app ask for camera or photo permissions?

Only to set a custom avatar for your player profile. You can always keep the default heraldic crest and decline the prompts.

How do I reset my progress?

Open the app, go to Settings → Erase All Data. This clears your profile, statistics, and trophies from the device. You can also offload or delete the app from iOS Settings.

Can I get a refund?

If you purchased the app through the App Store and are within Apple's refund window, request a refund through reportaproblem.apple.com. We're happy to help if you run into trouble — email us and we'll do our best.

Do prizes or tokens have monetary value?

No. All tokens, prizes, and rewards in the app are virtual scoring markers. They have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed for real currency or items.

I found a bug. How should I report it?

Email chekinaekaterina093@gmail.com with a description, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. A screen recording or screenshot is gold.

Is the app accessible?

Yes. The interface uses dynamic type, large hit targets, and VoiceOver-friendly labels on artwork.

Troubleshooting

  • The app feels slow after a long session: Quit and relaunch. iOS will reclaim background memory automatically.
  • My avatar disappeared: Erase All Data was triggered, or the photo-library permission was revoked. Re-grant access under iOS Settings and pick a new avatar.
  • Sound effects won't play: Check the in-app Settings toggle, the silent switch on the side of your iPhone, and the system volume.
  • Haptics feel weak: Some iPhones have a system-level haptic intensity toggle. The in-app Settings toggle can also be turned off.
